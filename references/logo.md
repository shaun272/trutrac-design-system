# Logo

Ten assets in `assets/`. The wordmark is **official artwork**, not type. Never typeset
"TRU-TRAC" in Archivo or anything else to stand in for the lockup: the letterforms, the spacing
and the ® are drawn, and a typeset substitute is wrong even when it looks close.

## The family

| File | Mark | Wordmark | Surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lockup-red.png` | red | red | light |
| `lockup-charcoal.png` | charcoal | charcoal | light |
| `lockup-red-reverse.png` | red, counterforms filled | white | dark |
| `lockup-charcoal-reverse.png` | charcoal, counterforms filled | white | dark |
| `lockup-white.png` | white | white | dark, single-colour, or over imagery |
| `mark-red.png` | red | — | light |
| `mark-charcoal.png` | charcoal | — | light |
| `mark-red-reverse.png` | red, counterforms filled | — | dark |
| `mark-charcoal-reverse.png` | charcoal, counterforms filled | — | dark |
| `mark-white.png` | white | — | dark, single-colour, embossing, engraving |

Lockup ratio 2561 : 671, roughly 3.82 : 1. Mark ratio 1152 : 1186, very close to square but
**not** square. Preserve both exactly.

## Which one

- **Documents, proposals, handbooks, reports.** `lockup-charcoal` on the cover and
  `mark-charcoal` in the running head. Charcoal, not red: the red is the accent, and a red
  lockup on every page spends it.
- **Red lockup.** Covers where the document is the brand statement, and the first page of a
  proposal. Not on interior pages.
- **Dark surfaces.** Always a `-reverse` variant. Never the light-surface file.
- **Single colour, engraving, embossing, fax-grade reproduction.** `-white` or `mark-charcoal`.

## Why reverse variants exist

Every file supplied by the brand is a knockout: the TRU TRAC lettering, the central disc and
the roller outline are transparent, not white. Dropped on a dark ground the counterforms fill
with that ground, the roller loses the outline separating it from the disc, and the mark reads
as a different logo. The reverse files were generated by flood-filling the exterior on the
alpha channel, isolating the interior counterforms, and painting them white beneath the
original artwork. The red and charcoal ink is untouched.

## Colour, and a discrepancy worth knowing

The supplied rasters carry **`#e42526`** and **`#e32726`** for red and **`#3a3a3c`** for
charcoal. The locked brand tokens are **`#E1261C`** (Pantone 485C) and **`#414042`**. They are
close and they are not the same.

**Never sample colour from a logo file.** Use the tokens. The rasters are artwork, not a
palette, and the difference is invisible until a sampled red sits next to a token red in the
same table.

## Sizing and clear space

| Application | Height |
|---|---|
| Document cover, lockup | 34 mm, roughly 128 px |
| Running head, mark | 9 mm, roughly 34 px |
| Presentation title slide, lockup | 64 px |
| Presentation footer, mark | 24 px |
| Web navigation, lockup | 40 px |
| Minimum, any application | 24 px, or 7 mm in print |

Clear space is one quarter of the mark height on all four sides, measured from the trimmed
edge of the artwork. Nothing enters it.

## Not permitted

- Typesetting the wordmark instead of using the lockup file.
- The light-surface file on a dark ground.
- Stretching. Both ratios are fixed.
- Rotating, skewing, recolouring, or applying a single flat colour other than the supplied white.
- A shadow, glow, outline, container or rounded plate behind the mark.
- The mark over a busy area of a photograph. Move it, or lay a solid band beneath it.

## Imagery pairing

Four photographic roles ship with the system in `assets/img/`: site, condition, technical and
application. All run one tonal grade, saturation 72 / contrast 106 / brightness 97, so adjacent
images on a spread read as one shoot. They are stock placeholders, held locally rather than
hot-linked so PDF generation works offline, and none of them shows a Tru-Trac installation.
Replace them with the Tru-Trac library and keep the grade.

The mark over a photograph always sits on a scrim and always over a quiet area of the frame.

## Open item

These are rasters at up to 3543 px. Comfortable to roughly 850 px on screen at 2× and to about
75 mm in print at 300 dpi. A document cover at 34 mm is fine; a full-bleed application is not.
A true vector master, with the counterforms expressed as white fills rather than knockouts,
should replace all ten when the brand pack is next opened.
